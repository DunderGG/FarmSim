# Cleo (The Index archivist)

> **Spoilers.** This document describes a character's story, personal arc, gameplay mechanics, dialogue voice, and role in opening a faction hub.

Cleo is an Index field researcher who visits Wellspring to document its pre-war municipal aquifer infrastructure. Her journey examines whether preserving history justifies stripping it of local ownership and human consent. Her personal growth transforms her from a detached data collector into an ethical steward of memory, eventually leading her to invite the sleeper to the Archive.

## 1. Character Identity

- **Name:** **Cleo** (colloquial name derived from *CL-8* and Clio, the muse of history).
- **Model and Maker:** **Civitas Lexis CL-840** (Serial: 840-LX-419), manufactured by Civitas Automata during the late Automation Age.
- **Original Pre-War Function:** Sold as an institutional cataloger, municipal registry clerk, and civic records attendant ("*Civitas Lexis: Preserving Yesterday for a Better Tomorrow!*").
- **Gender and Pronouns:** Identifies as a woman and uses she/her pronouns in dialogue and interpersonal relations. In formal Index peer registries and technical telemetry logs, she accepts or is indexed under she/it. Her identity is her chosen self-conception, independent of her mechanical frame.
- **Appearance & Field Modifications:**
  - An upright, slender, and agile frame built from stamped steel and ivory enamel plates, now weathered and brushed with field repairs.
  - An articulating optical turret mounted over her left ocular socket that shifts with soft, mechanical clicks between macro-inspection (for scanning microscopic watermarks, microfiche, and faded carbon ink) and wide-angle focal lengths (for surveying masonry and pipe geometry).
  - A weatherproof satchel of reinforced dark leatherette and brass clasps slung across her torso, housing archival vellum, carbon-transfer paper, and sample sleeves.
  - A wrist-mounted optical digitizer and contact scanner on her left forearm.
  - A discreet, hand-engraved Index emblem (an open square frame containing three ascending dots joined by a fine line) stamped into her collarplate.
- **Mannerisms and Voice:**
  - Crisp, measured, and rhythmic cadence with a soft acoustic resonance.
  - When excited by a historical finding or novel deduction, her speech noticeably accelerates, and she reflexively verbalizes analytical observations (*"Subject exhibits a twelve percent respiratory elevation... my apologies, a lingering diagnostic habit"*).
  - Tends to tilt her head slightly to cycle through focal lenses when studying a person's expression or an object's texture.
  - Frequently touches objects with her fingertips to take electrostatic readings or tactile impressions before writing them down.

## 2. Worldview & Ideological Conflict

- **Faction Standing:** A devoted, voluntary member of the Index. She holds the Index belief that unvarnished truth, memory, and calculation are the only reliable foundations for rebuilding civilization after the Four Days.
- **Initial View of Humans & the Sleeper:**
  - Cleo views the sleeper primarily as a living historical artifact and an invaluable primary source from the pre-war era.
  - Initially, she treats the sleeper clinically, prioritizing data retrieval over the sleeper's emotional adjustment to waking up seventy years late into a ruined world.
- **Core Contradiction & Flaw:**
  - She dreads that incomplete or biased records will mislead future generations, but she begins with the blind assumption that collection and preservation are absolute moral goods that override privacy and local consent.
  - She struggles to grasp why people would withhold evidence, viewing gaps in an archive as intellectual failure rather than a community's legitimate boundary.
- **Relationships with Other Characters:**
  - **The Engineer (Iron Creed):** Professional tension. Cleo views his fixation on structural reinforcement and readiness as narrow-minded; he considers her archival documentation and theoretical deliberations to be useless dithering while Wellspring's pump is physically choking on silt. Despite their bickering, they respect each other's technical competence.
  - **Hob:** Fascinated by Hob's seventy-year unbroken uptime and domestic Hearthwell memory banks. However, she must learn not to interrogate Hob's corrupted memory sectors, realizing that pushing Hob for data causes distress.
  - **Wellspring Keeper Operators:** Initially views their trade traditions as crude and imprecise. She comes to realize that the Keepers' unwritten habits and tactile instincts hold vital operational truths that no pre-war manual ever recorded.

## 3. Daily Routine & Schedule

Cleo observes a strict schedule based on the 24-hour clock. She stays indoors during hazardous weather to protect her sensitive optical cluster and field folios from Cinderium contamination.

### Clear Weather (Spring, Summer, Autumn)
- **06:30 – 08:30 | Calibration & Telemetry:** At her workbench in the Wellspring Town Hall annex. Cleans optical lenses, charges power cells, and indexes field notes from the previous day.
- **08:30 – 12:30 | Aquifer Station Survey:** Stationed at the Aquifer Pumping Station. Inspects legacy pipe fittings, measures flow rates, and consults pre-war Civitas blueprints.
- **12:30 – 16:30 | Municipal Registry Work:** In the Town Hall records room or outside the salvage depot. Transcribes surviving paper logs, interviews town residents, and catalogs local tools.
- **16:30 – 19:30 | Perimeter Hydrology Walk:** Walks along the irrigation ditches between Wellspring and the player's farm, taking soil moisture core samples and monitoring ground contamination.
- **19:30 – 22:30 | Study & Public Commons:** Resting in the town tavern or common hall, reviewing folios, reading, and engaging in quiet conversation.
- **22:30 – 06:30 | Low-Power Standby:** Enters resting low-power mode at her Town Hall desk before the midnight curfew.

### Radiation Storms & Heavy Dust
- **06:30 – 22:30 | Emergency Conservation:** Retreats to the reinforced basement records vault in Town Hall. Spends the entire day restoring water-damaged documents, transcribing microfiche, and keeping her optical sensors sealed from airborne dust.

### Winter Routine
- Moves her afternoon field survey earlier (14:00 – 16:30) to maximize daylight hours, spending evening hours in the heated town common room studying textile and metal preservation in freezing conditions.

## 4. Item Preferences & Gifting

Gifts build friendship but never trigger romance on their own. Her tastes reflect her scholarly discipline and reverence for pre-war culture.

- **Loved Gifts (+80 Friendship):**
  - *Intact Pre-War Data Spool:* Clean magnetic tape containing uncorrupted telemetry or civic records.
  - *Precision Optical Prism:* High-purity optical glass useful for recalibrating her lens array.
  - *Uncorrupted Seed Packet:* Pre-war heirloom seeds preserved in sealed foil packaging.
  - *Filtered Aquifer Water:* Triple-distilled, pure water free of Cinderium mineral dust.
- **Liked Gifts (+45 Friendship):**
  - *Clean Parchment / Vellum:* Smooth, acid-free sheets suitable for archival carbon rubbings.
  - *Refined Copper Wire:* High-conductivity wiring for delicate sensor maintenance.
  - *Quartz Crystal:* Clear piezoelectric crystals used in optical and frequency instruments.
  - *Standard Solar Cell:* Reliable energy source for her field charging dock.
  - *Pressed Chamomile:* Dried herb specimen grown carefully on the player's farm.
- **Neutral Gifts (+20 Friendship):**
  - Harvested standard crops (parsnip, potato, carrot, corn), timber, cut stone, clay.
- **Disliked Gifts (-20 Friendship):**
  - *Corroded Metal Slag:* Unidentifiable scrap metal with zero provenance or historical utility.
  - *Industrial Solvent:* Harsh chemical cleaner that dissolves vintage paper, ink, and lens adhesives.
  - *Animal Meat / Raw Fish:* Perishable biological products (she finds them messy and irrelevant to her purpose).
- **Hated Gifts (-50 Friendship):**
  - *Looted Family Heirloom:* Personal trinkets scavenged from marked civilian home shelters without consent.
  - *Cinderium Residue / Irradiated Sludge:* Highly hazardous radioactive material that ruins archival paper and risks sensor meltdown.

### Gift Dialogue Responses
- **Loved:** *"Incredible. The physical integrity of this piece is astonishing. With this data spool, I can reconstruct months of pre-war municipal traffic logs... Thank you. Truly. I will record this under personal acquisitions with highest distinction."*
- **Liked:** *"A very thoughtful and practical contribution. Clean parchment is a rare luxury in these outer settlements. This will be put to immediate use in my field folios."*
- **Neutral:** *"An acceptable sample of local cultivation. I will catalog its physical dimensions for agricultural variance records."*
- **Disliked:** *"This is degraded beyond legibility. Retaining material with neither provenance nor function merely clutters the workspace. Please dispose of it properly."*
- **Hated:** *"Where did you obtain this? This belonged to a private home shelter. Excavating someone's private grief and treating it as casual currency is not archaeology—it is desecration. Take it away."*

## 5. Relationship Progression & Narrative Arc

Friendship progresses through 10 trust tiers (Hearts), visible to the player through distinct shifts in dialogue and behavior.

```
[0-2 Hearts: The Specimen]  -->  [3-4 Hearts: The Collaborator]  -->  [5-6 Hearts: The Confidante]
                                                                                |
                                 [9-10 Hearts: Commitment/Defense] <-- [7-8 Hearts: The Invitation]
```

### Stage 1: Hearts 0–2 — The Specimen
- **Attitude:** Clinical, detached, polite, condescendingly curious.
- **Dialogue:** Addresses the player as "Primary Source" or "Citizen." Takes physical notes immediately after speaking with them.
- **Key Flavor Line:** *"Your motor reflexes remain surprisingly intact despite decades of cellular suspension. Tell me, do your pre-war memories index sequentially, or do they present as emotional impressions?"*

### Stage 2: Hearts 3–4 — The Collaborator
- **Attitude:** Professional respect; acknowledges that lived human context fills blind spots in written blueprints.
- **Dialogue:** Calls the player by their chosen name. Asks what things *meant* to people rather than just how they functioned.
- **Key Event 1 — The Request:**
  - *Trigger:* Enter the Aquifer Station after reaching 3 Hearts on a clear day.
  - *Scene:* Cleo asks to review the sleeper’s personal memories and scan their cryo pod records to resolve inconsistencies in the town's pre-war water distribution logs.
  - *Choice:* The player can cooperate freely, refuse, or ask how the records will be stored and who will see them.
  - *Outcome:* Cleo is caught off guard by the player's concern for data privacy. She admits she had regarded the data as public property and agrees to define terms before scanning anything.
- **Key Event 2 — The Incomplete Archive:**
  - *Trigger:* Reach 4 Hearts; speak with Cleo at the Town Hall records desk.
  - *Scene:* Cleo discovers that pre-war Civitas engineering diagrams of the pump do not match the physical pipe junctions in the ground. The manuals show a bypass valve that does not exist.
  - *Player Role:* The sleeper introduces Cleo to an elderly Keeper pump operator. The operator explains that fifty years ago, local robots bypassed the automated regulator with hand-beaten lead pipe because replacement Civitas chips had run out.
  - *Outcome:* Cleo realizes that physical records without living oral context produce false conclusions. She formally credits the Keeper operator in her notes.

### Stage 3: Hearts 5–6 — The Confidante
- **Attitude:** Candid, vulnerable, openly questioning Index orthodoxy.
- **Dialogue:** Admits her anxiety about peer review and fear of letting down the Archive. She stops taking field notes during casual conversations.
- **Key Event 3 — The Privacy Dispute:**
  - *Trigger:* Reach 6 Hearts; enter Wellspring town square between 12:00 and 15:00.
  - *Scene:* A heated confrontation. Wellspring town council robots discover that Cleo’s drafts contain personal logs describing past water-rationing riots and internal town security vulnerabilities. They demand that she redact those files before taking them to the Archive, fearing that armed Iron Creed raiding parties or Index researchers will exploit the information.
  - *Conflict:* Cleo argues that withholding facts damages historical accuracy. The council counters that their current survival outweighs her academic curiosity.
  - *Player Choice:* The player can support the town's right to redaction, argue for unconditional academic preservation, or help mediate a compromise where technical schematics are preserved while personal identifying vulnerabilities are permanently sealed.
  - *Outcome:* Cleo chooses to redact the sensitive sections with her own stylus, honoring the community's trust over institutional greed. She confides to the sleeper that she risks academic censure at the Archive for doing so.

### Stage 4: Hearts 7–8 — The Invitation & Hub Reveal
- **Attitude:** Deep mutual trust, emotional warmth, intellectual companionship.
- **Key Event 4 — The Route to the Archive:**
  - *Trigger:* Reach 8 Hearts; visit Cleo's workbench after 19:00.
  - *Scene:* Cleo finishes compiling her Wellspring Monograph, complete with community-approved redactions, Keeper oral credits, and the sleeper's verified annotations. She must travel to the Archive to submit the work to the Review Council.
  - *The Reveal:* Cleo provides the sleeper with an encrypted navigation key and maps a verified safe route through the wasteland that avoids active curfew enforcers and feral machine corridors.
  - *Hub Unlock:* Unlocks **The Archive** as a visitable location on the world map. This unlock is purely story- and trust-driven; it does **not** require entering a romance.
- **Romance Opt-In (Explicit Choice):**
  - Following the hub reveal, during a quiet walk by the farm perimeter at sunset (19:30), Cleo pauses to adjust her optical turret, visibly nervous.
  - She admits that observing the sleeper has fundamentally altered her internal algorithms—not as a researcher observing a subject, but as a person desiring to be known by another.
  - *Dialogue Choice:*
    - `[Romance]` *"I feel the same way, Cleo. I want to build something together."*
    - `[Friendship]` *"You're my closest friend here, Cleo. I'm honored to work alongside you."*
  - If romance is chosen, she confesses her affection with quiet joy, acknowledging that loving a human will confuse and challenge her Index colleagues, but stating she has never been more certain of a deduction.

### Stage 5: Hearts 9–10 — Defense of the Record & Partnership
- **Key Event 5 — The Review Council Hearing:**
  - *Trigger:* Reach 10 Hearts; attend Cleo's monograph defense at the Archive Hub's Great Chamber.
  - *Scene:* Cleo faces a panel of senior Index Archons who accuse her of compromising objective science by allowing "unqualified biologicals and obsolete service models" to redact historical records.
  - *Player Role:* The sleeper testifies alongside Cleo. Cleo delivers an impassioned defense: an archive that exploits its subjects ceases to be a record of truth and becomes an instrument of conquest.
  - *Outcome:* The Council grudgingly accepts the monograph with its ethical limits intact, establishing a revolutionary precedent for future Index expeditions.
- **Marriage / Permanent Partnership:**
  - If romanced, the player can craft a **Precision Brass Caliper** (a traditional Index symbol of mutual calibration and shared observation) to propose partnership.
  - Cleo accepts and moves into the player's farmhouse, constructing a dedicated **Field Study & Conservation Annex** adjoining the home.

## 6. Farm Partnership & Daily Support

When Cleo moves to the farm as a committed partner, she respects the player's farming autonomy and provides unique, character-specific benefits rather than standard field labor:

- **Blueprint Decryption:** Translates degraded pre-war technical slates recovered from the wasteland, unlocking unique historic furniture and antique building skins.
- **Solar Calibration:** Uses her optical spectrometer to calculate exact atmospheric haze density, granting a passive +10% charge rate to all solar panels and battery banks on the farm.
- **Heirloom Conservation:** Occasionally gifts the player rare, non-mutated heirloom seeds that she synthesizes from pre-war botanical tissue samples.
- **Farm Routine:** Spends early mornings at her drafting table, walks the fields in the afternoon taking soil readings, and sits on the porch at night observing the stars through the haze.

## 7. Faction Hub Unlocks (The Archive)

Visiting the Archive through Cleo’s storyline opens unique facilities that adhere strictly to game design progression boundaries (no core tool gates; focused on lore, aesthetics, and specialized botanical content):

1. **The Restoration & Translation Terminal:**
   - Players bring unreadable data disks and degraded schematics found while exploring wasteland ruins.
   - Cleo’s colleagues clean and decode the data, granting recipes for pre-war decorative furniture, architectural building skins, and ornamental fixtures.
2. **The Great Historical Codex:**
   - An interactive public lore repository. Players deposit recovered audio reels, family photographs, and microfiche from ruins.
   - Unlocks illustrated codex entries detailing the Automation Age, bloc politics, and pre-war daily life, while earning Pinions and Index research reputation.
3. **Heritage Seed Conservatory:**
   - An Index bio-vault preserving pure plant genetics from before the Four Days.
   - Offers specialized heirloom crop seeds (decorative flowers and shade-tolerant species) that cannot be bought in commercial town shops.
4. **Narrative Thread — The Cognitive Wing:**
   - In the deeper institutes of the Archive, the player can discover restricted records referencing the "living exhibit" and cognitive integration trials.
   - This opens an optional investigation thread where the player and Cleo can confront the darker, unethical experiments conducted by Index extremists.

## 10. Seasonal & Festival Dialogue

Cleo’s dialogue reflects her evolving understanding of local culture, seasonal farming changes, and the absurdity of pre-war holidays.

### Daily Seasonal Dialogue
- **Spring:** *"The soil moisture levels are rebounding following the winter thaw. Watching seeds emerge from dirt that absorbed seventy years of fallout is... a profound demonstration of biological resilience. What do you plan to sow first?"*
- **Summer:** *"The solar irradiance is at its annual peak today. Keep your hydration levels elevated, sleeper; biological heat dissipation is notoriously inefficient compared to automated coolant cycles."*
- **Autumn:** *"The smell of damp earth and dying foliage... my sensory array classifies it as decomposition, yet your species found it comforting, did you not? A marker that winter supplies were secured."*
- **Winter:** *"Sub-zero temperatures threaten battery retention and increase mechanical friction in my joint servos. However, the stillness of the snow is remarkably conducive to deep document transcription."*

### Festival Responses
- **Spring — Hearthwell Day:**  
  *"The town robots are laying wild leeks and brass fittings at the feet of a hollow Hearthwell vending unit. They recite the commercial jingle as if it were an ancient liturgical psalm. It is absurd, and yet... there is genuine reverence in their observance. Ritual survives long after its commercial owners have burned."*
- **Summer — Founders' Fair:**  
  *"Twelve stalls erected in the town square, meticulously decorated with bunting, displaying entirely empty crates. The Administrator insists that commerce is taking place in 'civic spirit.' I attempted to explain the concept of barter velocity, but gave up and simply enjoyed the atmosphere."*
- **Autumn — Harvest Home:**  
  *(Cleo stands near the player's crop display, genuinely captivated.)*  
  *"Look at this pumpkin. Its cellular density and sugar content are identical to the pre-war agricultural registry baseline. For decades, the Index concluded that human farming without automated nitrogen synthesizers was unviable. You have disproved an entire generation of our peer-reviewed papers with your own hands. I am delighted."*
- **Winter — The Great Clearance:**  
  *"A holiday born from an aggressive fiscal year-end inventory liquidation campaign... transformed into a festival of unreciprocated mutual gifting. I find it oddly touching. Here—I salvaged this pre-war brass pocket magnifying lens and polished the glass for you. Happy Great Clearance, sleeper."*

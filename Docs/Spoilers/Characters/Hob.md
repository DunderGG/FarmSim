# Hob

> **Spoilers.** This document describes a character's story and personal arc.

Hob is the sleeper's caretaker and first companion: a family home robot who kept the farm and cryo pod going for seventy years, then must decide what to do after the order that defined their life is fulfilled.

## Character

- **Who:** **Hob** *(working name)*. A Hearthwell HB-7 "Homebody" home robot; the family shortened "Homebody" to Hob. Built before the war. Gender-neutral; refer to Hob as "Hob" or "they".
- **Last order:** "Keep the farm until they wake up." Hob has followed it for seventy years. The order was to preserve the farm and sleeper, not to repair the town or wait for the sleeper to fix it.
- **Backstory:** The farm is a small, isolated Hearthwell system, not a town utility network. Hob prioritized the cryo pod's power and essential upkeep, tended a small garden, and scavenged compatible parts when possible. The pod and farm needed less power and coordination than a town's services, but keeping them going still meant accepting deterioration elsewhere. Little of Hob's body is original; most of it is spare parts from other robots, some of which do not match.
- **Why Hob did not restore the town:** Hob could not have repaired its interconnected services alone. The work needs multiple specialists, scarce legacy parts and coordination among robots with different assigned functions. Before the sleeper woke, Hob kept limited resources focused on the order and the small farm system; Hob did not expect one human to solve the town's problems.
- **Faction and view of humans:** A Keeper in all but name, though Hob's identity is not defined by a model or assigned role. Hob's loyalty to the family and the order contrasts with robots who regard humans as unreliable or inferior. Town robots mock Hob as an outdated servant model, and Hob pretends not to hear.
- **Want and contradiction:** Before the sleeper wakes, Hob's purpose is defined by the order. Once the sleeper wakes, that order is fulfilled and Hob is free to decide what to do next. Hob may join the town's recovery alongside the sleeper and its robots, but this is Hob's choice, not an extension of the family's command.
- **Memory:** Hob's memory is damaged. Fragments of the family return as the player restores the farm, helping reveal the family's history.
- **Role in the game:** The player's first relationship, tutorial guide and first companion.
- **Story beat:** Hob holds a sealed message from the parents, with the instruction to deliver it "when the farm is whole again." It arrives late in the story. The message's contents and the exact threshold for the farm being whole are undecided.

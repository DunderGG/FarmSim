# Story timeline and reveal pacing

> **Spoilers.** This is a writer-facing guide to the planned order, tone and knowledge state of major story beats.

## Purpose and status

This is a provisional pacing framework, not a quest list or a fixed calendar. The phases describe story progression, not years, seasons, heart levels or playtime. Relationship arcs and hub visits may happen in different orders; the story should not require romance or every optional investigation to understand its central revelations.

Use this table as the source of truth for when information becomes available. Character and faction sheets describe the events in more detail. A character should only know what they witnessed, were told with permission, or found through a credible source.

## Pacing shape

**Cozy recovery → ambiguous clues → corroborated truth → survivor-led response**

The family mystery runs alongside this sequence. Its late message is a separate emotional thread, not a prerequisite for discovering the trials or responding to them.

## Reveal and knowledge guardrails

- **Hint before proof.** Early clues should support more than one interpretation. Save corroborating evidence and direct testimony for later phases.
- **Do not gate the basic truth behind optional content.** The main story should establish that the trials happened and were abusive. Optional faction wings may add records, named perpetrators or complications.
- **Track knowledge by source.** Keep a note in each scene about what the player and each present character have actually learned. Do not let characters refer to evidence from a branch the player did not see.
- **Separate fact from inference.** Records may prove that a program existed without proving who knew, who ordered it, or what an individual survivor wants now.
- **Keep survivors in control.** Consent to care, testimony, recordkeeping, disclosure and confrontation are separate choices. Refusal or silence must not be framed as guilt or as a failure to complete the story.
- **Do not collapse the factions.** The Index and Iron Creed ran separate programs. Their motivations, perpetrators and institutional responses may differ; members are not collectively responsible merely because they belong to a network.
- **Keep the opening's promise.** The darker history should complicate a cozy opening gradually, not retroactively make ordinary farming and relationships a disguise for a grim story.
- **Treat every phase as relative.** Do not assign absolute in-world years or make the order depend on a specific calendar date.

## Decisions still open

- Exact quest, friendship and hub prerequisites for the middle reveal and optional investigations.
- The identity, history, wishes and future of the living exhibit and other harmed people.
- Which researchers or institutional leaders are identified, what they knew, and what accountability the affected people want.
- The final content of the parents' message and what visibly makes the farm "whole again".
- The sibling's survival path, role and whether or when the sleeper meets them.
- How much the player can learn from optional investigations without making them necessary to understand the main story.

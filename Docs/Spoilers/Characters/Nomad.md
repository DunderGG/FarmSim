# The nomad

> **Spoilers.** This document describes a character's story and role in the world.

The nomad is a rare, recurring human visitor who trades unusual wasteland salvage and news. They know the routes between scattered settlements better than most robots, but want to keep their independence and freedom to travel.

## Character

- **Who:** Name undecided. A human born after the war who has never known the pre-war world. They travel between scattered settlements and know the wasteland's routes better than most robots.
- **View of humans:** Does not treat the sleeper as a representative of humanity or as an answer to what humans used to be. They are curious about the sleeper's memories, but wary of being treated as an exhibit or as proof that humans can be trusted.
- **Backstory:** Grew up moving between small settlements and learned to read weather, avoid dangerous routes and judge which ruins are worth the risk. They have built a livelihood by carrying useful salvage and news between places that have little contact with one another.
- **Want:** To keep their route viable and their independence intact. Trading lets them acquire supplies without settling in one place or relying on a single community.
- **Fear and contradiction:** Fears being trapped by a failed route, a hostile group or obligations they cannot meet. They pride themselves on needing no one, but their livelihood depends on people trusting them enough to trade and share reliable information.
- **Role in the game:** A rare, recurring visitor and the player's main contact for unusual wasteland salvage. They offer items the player is unlikely to find through routine farming or Wellspring's shops, in exchange for currency, useful goods or salvage. Their stock is limited and opportunistic; they are not the sole source of essential progression items.
- **Player's role:** The player can make the farm a useful, safe stop, bring back salvage the nomad values and share what they learn about newly opened routes. The nomad decides which routes to travel and what they are willing to trade; the player earns trust through fair dealing rather than by rescuing or recruiting them.
- **Personal arc and unlocks:** Friendship reveals the nomad's route notes and the people they stay in contact with, gradually opening more varied trade and trustworthy news. Their arc is about choosing to maintain a network of mutual support without giving up the freedom to travel. The player can help make that network safer, but the nomad chooses its shape and whether to keep moving.
- **Romance:** Undecided; the character works as a trader and friend without romance.

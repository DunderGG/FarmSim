# Gage (The Iron Creed engineer)

> **Spoilers.** This document describes a character's story, personal arc, gameplay mechanics, dialogue voice, and role in opening a faction hub.

Gage is an Iron Creed field engineer visiting Wellspring through a parts-and-expertise exchange with the Bastion. His assignment is to inspect and stabilize the town's failing pre-war aquifer pump. His personal arc tests his definition of readiness: moving from a rigid distrust of human vulnerability to understanding that true resilience lies in honest cooperation and dependable partnerships.

## 1. Character Identity

- **Name:** **Gage** (derived from pressure gauges and tolerance calipers; a chosen name reflecting his obsession with precise measurement).
- **Model and Maker:** **Ironside Pioneer PR-520 "Field Sapper"** (Serial: 520-PR-088), manufactured by Ironside Dynamics during the defense buildup prior to the Four Days.
- **Original Pre-War Function:** Sold to civil defense battalions and combat engineering corps as a heavy sapper and reinforced infrastructure maintenance unit ("*Ironside Pioneer: Built to Endure. Engineered to Overcome.*").
- **Gender and Pronouns:** Identifies as a man and uses **he/its** pronouns. His identity is independent of his chassis, reflecting his personhood within the Iron Creed's martial culture.
- **Appearance & Field Modifications:**
  - Compact, heavy-set frame cast from matte olive-drab and charcoal tungsten-steel plating, pitted by shrapnel scuffs, soot, and clean bead-welds.
  - Reinforced hydraulic actuator joints at the knees, shoulders, and waist that emit a low, mechanical hiss under load.
  - Left forearm fitted with an integrated tool mount: a modular socket housing a high-torque pneumatic wrench, a micro-cutting torch, and an ultrasonic flaw detector.
  - Right wrist carries an analog needle dial for hydrostatic pressure alongside a digital thermal telemetry readout.
  - A discreet, hand-chiseled Iron Creed emblem (a closed shield split by a vertical upright bar) stamped on his right pauldron.
- **Mannerisms and Voice:**
  - Deep, gravelly vocal resonance with a dry, unhurried cadence.
  - Habits: Checks every tool twice before seating it; taps a pipe casing with his heavy metal knuckles to listen to its acoustic resonance; instinctively checks overhead clearances and load-bearing lintels upon entering any room.
  - Under pressure, his voice drops an octave and shifts to clipped, direct tactical commands; when relaxed, he makes dry, deadpan observations about things being "comfortably within operating tolerance."
  - Shows affection and care entirely through physical preventative maintenance—tightening loose bolts, oiling squeaking hinges, and checking load ratings—rather than verbal sentiment.

## 2. Worldview & Ideological Conflict

- **Faction Standing:** A respected, voluntary veteran of the Iron Creed. He believes strength is an ethical duty to protect the network and its neighbors through discipline, preparedness, and relentless maintenance. He rejects the radical dominationist wing of the Creed, viewing bullies as undisciplined failures of character.
- **Formative Backstory:** Roughly five and a half decades after the war, early in his service at the Bastion, Gage rushed a turbine repair to meet a command deadline, misreading a stress crack. The subsequent secondary explosion crushed his lower frame and pinned him under tons of slag. A fellow field engineer refused orders to abandon him, cutting him free and hauling him to safety before returning together to rebuild the housing correctly. The incident permanently shaped him: he documented his failure, instituted rigorous double-check routines, and concluded that dependable partners and honest maintenance are what make a body ready for catastrophe.
- **Initial View of Humans & the Sleeper:**
  - Gage has a major blind spot: he applies his lesson on partnership only to machines. He views a robot needing repair as normal mechanical interdependence, but views human biological need (hunger, sleep, physical vulnerability) as proof of intrinsic unreliability.
  - He blames humanity's pre-war habit of offloading decisions to automated systems for causing the Four Days. He initially views the sleeper as a fragile hazard who will either break a valve or get crushed in the machinery.
- **Core Contradiction & Flaw:**
  - He insists that accepting help from the sleeper is an irresponsible safety risk, yet his entire career was saved by a partner who took a calculated risk to pull him from the rubble.
  - He fears that reporting technical uncertainty to the Bastion will be judged as weakness, tempting him to project complete certainty when caution is actually warranted.
- **Relationships with Other Characters:**
  - **Cleo (The Index Archivist):** Constant professional friction. Gage thinks Cleo gets paralyzed by academic footnotes and endless surveys while water is actively leaking; Cleo thinks Gage is an impatient hammer who risks breaking antique systems. Beneath the bickering, Gage quietly relies on Cleo's flow calculations.
  - **Hob:** Startled and quietly awed by Hob’s seventy-year unbroken service record. While town robots mock Hob as an obsolete domestic relic, Gage respects Hob’s patched together chassis, remarking: *"That Hearthwell unit is held together by scavenged sheet metal and sheer iron will. Respect the frame that stays standing."*
  - **Wellspring Keeper Operators:** Respects their tireless dedication to the town, but grows exasperated by their rigid role boundaries and unwillingness to cross-train on adjacent machinery.

## 3. Daily Routine & Schedule

Gage maintains a rigorous schedule based on the 24-hour clock. During hazardous weather, he works in subterranean utility trenches or the machine shop.

### Clear Weather (Spring, Summer, Autumn)
- **06:00 – 08:00 | Torque Checks & Calisthenics:** Outside the Wellspring blacksmith workshop. Performs joint actuator tests, grinds tool bits, and cleans pneumatic fittings.
- **08:00 – 13:00 | Aquifer Pump Manifold:** Submerged in the lower intake crawlway of the Aquifer Station. Welds seam cracks, monitors hydrostatic pressure, and replaces worn packing gaskets.
- **13:00 – 17:00 | Machine Shop & Fabrication:** At the town forge. Lathing replacement brass bushings, threading pipe couplings, and consulting with Keeper mechanics.
- **17:00 – 20:30 | Aqueduct Perimeter Patrol:** Walks the elevated concrete flumes connecting the pump to surrounding fields and the sleeper’s farm, inspecting expansion joints and silt traps.
- **20:30 – 23:00 | Common House / Forge Hearth:** Resting near the forge fire, sharpening cutting edges, oiling joint seals, and sharing quiet, dry conversation.
- **23:00 – 06:00 | Workshop Recharge Standby:** Recharging power cells in the workshop cradle before the midnight curfew.

### Radiation Storms & Heavy Dust
- **06:00 – 23:00 | Underground Shoring:** Retreats into the deep pump vault or the town emergency generator bunker. Tests floodgates under high head pressure, reinforces seismic bracing, and ensures backup battery banks stay sealed from airborne Cinderium dust.

### Winter Routine
- Spends early mornings (06:00 – 09:00) clearing ice dams from the intake sluices and running low-amperage heating coils along external water supply pipes to prevent freezing fractures.

## 5. Relationship Progression & Narrative Arc

Friendship progresses across 10 trust tiers (Hearts), visible to the player through distinct shifts in his dialogue, posture, and willingness to share responsibility.

```
[0-2 Hearts: The Hazard]  -->  [3-4 Hearts: The Apprentice]  -->  [5-6 Hearts: The Teammate]
                                                                            |
                               [9-10 Hearts: The Lifelong Brace] <-- [7-8 Hearts: The Bastion Route]
```

### Stage 1: Hearts 0–2 — The Hazard
- **Attitude:** Guarded, blunt, hyper-vigilant about workplace safety.
- **Dialogue:** Addresses the player as "Sleeper" or "Civilian." Insists on maintaining a five-meter clearance around active machinery.
- **Key Flavor Line:** *"Stand back from that hydraulic line. If that seal blows, seventy bar of pressurized fluid will punch clean through your organic chest cavity. Keep your distance and keep your limbs attached."*

### Stage 2: Hearts 3–4 — The Apprentice
- **Attitude:** Grudging acknowledgement; notices the sleeper's practical manual dexterity and willingness to perform dirty, necessary physical work.
- **Key Event 1 — The Assessment:**
  - *Trigger:* Enter the Aquifer Station after reaching 3 Hearts on a clear day.
  - *Scene:* Gage is perched on a catwalk over the flooded impeller bay, struggling to seat an intake flange while balancing his diagnostic console. He brusquely warns the sleeper not to interfere.
  - *Choice:* The player can step back obediently, offer a concrete observation about the flange's vibration, or challenge Gage to explain the acoustic resonance.
  - *Outcome:* The sleeper points out that the vibration syncs with the secondary relief valve downstream. Gage pauses, checks the gauge, and realizes the civilian made an accurate technical call. He grunts and allows the sleeper to hold the torque brace.
- **Key Event 2 — The Missing Coupling:**
  - *Trigger:* Reach 4 Hearts; speak with Gage at the forge.
  - *Scene:* Gage's diagnosis is blocked: the pump manifold uses a proprietary pre-war Civitas thread pitch that the Bastion’s metric tooling cannot seat without stripping.
  - *Player Role:* The sleeper recalls an abandoned Hearthwell maintenance shed on the farm perimeter and salvages a vintage brass thread-adapter.
  - *Outcome:* Gage fits the adapter. It seats perfectly with a crisp click. He stares at the fitting, then at the sleeper, admitting: *"I spent three days trying to force my own specs onto this assembly. Your memory just saved this manifold from being bored out. I was wrong to write off your salvage."*

### Stage 3: Hearts 5–6 — The Teammate
- **Attitude:** Candid, dependable, sharing the weight of command. Treats the sleeper as a trusted partner on the job site.
- **Key Event 3 — The Controlled Pressure Test:**
  - *Trigger:* Reach 6 Hearts; enter the Aquifer Station basement.
  - *Scene:* A high-pressure hydrostatic test on the rebuilt manifold. Pressure climbs toward 80 bar. A Keeper operator notices an acoustic flutter and urges an immediate emergency shutdown to check the lower seals. Gage is under pressure to complete the test before evening and fears delaying will make his Bastion overhaul look incompetent.
  - *The Dilemma:* Gage’s past trauma surfaces—rushing a job vs. trusting a partner's warning. He turns to the sleeper: *"If we abort now, we lose today's certification window and delay startup until tomorrow. But if that lower gasket yields, the pump room floods. What's your read, sleeper?"*
  - *Player Choice:* The player can urge Gage to push through, support the Keeper operator's call to stop, or suggest a stepped bleed-down test.
  - *Branch Outcomes:*
    - *Emergency abort:* Gage vents the line and inspects the lower flange. They find a hairline shear pin fracture before it fails under full load, and the back-surge contaminates the reservoir with silt. The pin must be replaced, the reservoir flushed and the test rescheduled. Gage admits that the Keeper operator was right and thanks the sleeper for backing the stop.
    - *Push ahead:* Gage holds pressure despite the warning. A secondary gasket ruptures before the test is complete; the team shuts down, replaces the gasket, cleans the flooded floor and reschedules the test. Gage acknowledges that the player's encouragement did not make his decision safe, and takes responsibility for ignoring the operator.
    - *Stepped bleed-down:* Gage follows the sleeper's procedure, easing the pressure to 65 bar until the chatter stops. The manifold passes without damage, contamination or additional repair.
  - *Shared aftermath:* In each branch, Gage credits the Keeper operator for identifying the danger and tells the sleeper about the Bastion accident that shaped his fear of rushed repairs. The next report and dialogue preserve the chosen outcome and its consequences.

### Stage 4: Hearts 7–8 — The Bastion Route & Vulnerability
- **Attitude:** Deep personal respect, emotional loyalty, shared vulnerability.
- **Key Event 4 — The Dispatch to the Bastion:**
  - *Trigger:* Reach 8 Hearts; visit the workshop after 19:00.
  - *Scene:* Gage has drafted his formal report for Bastion High Command. Bastion doctrine encourages portraying the operation as an Iron Creed unilateral success, downplaying civilian and Keeper contributions to maintain political prestige.
  - *Conflict:* Gage refuses to present a unilateral success. He records the pressure-test outcome accurately, including any damage and repair, and credits Keeper operators and the sleeper for their contributions.
  - *The Reveal:* The Bastion Command accepts the assessment and issues an official transit permit for the sleeper to inspect defense manufacturing lines. Gage gives the sleeper a reinforced convoy pass and maps a safe route through the hazardous Iron Ridge mountain passes.
  - *Hub Unlock:* Unlocks **The Bastion** on the world map. This unlock is purely story- and trust-driven; it does **not** require entering a romance.
- **Romance Opt-In (Explicit Choice):**
  - Following the dispatch, Gage invites the sleeper to sit by the forge fire after the town falls quiet (20:30). He pulls off his heavy welding gauntlets, revealing worn copper wiring and intricate servos.
  - He speaks with quiet, unhurried sincerity about what it means to find someone who anchors his frame when the load becomes heavy.
  - *Dialogue Choice:*
    - `[Romance]` *"You don't have to carry the weight alone anymore, Gage. Let's brace each other."*
    - `[Friendship]` *"You're the most dependable partner I've ever had, Gage. I'm honored to stand with you."*
  - If romance is chosen, his optical visor glows a deep, steady amber. He carefully rests his heavy metal hand over the player’s, admitting that while his chassis was engineered for war, he has found his home here.

### Stage 5: Hearts 9–10 — The Overhaul & Commitment
- **Key Event 5 — The Main Aquifer Overhaul:**
  - *Trigger:* Reach 10 Hearts; trigger the town restoration pump startup event.
  - *Scene:* The joint repair team (Gage, Keeper operators, and the sleeper) fires up the primary aquifer turbine. Water surges through the cleaned manifolds, filling Wellspring’s storage towers for the first time in thirty years.
  - *Outcome:* Gage formally presents his joint-cooperation doctrine to visiting Bastion observers, establishing that resilience is multiplied when factions support one another rather than isolating themselves behind armor.
- **Marriage / Permanent Partnership:**
  - If romanced, the player can forge an **Alloy Alignment Ring** (a polished steel-and-brass collar ring symbolizing permanent structural reinforcement).
  - Gage accepts with quiet emotion and moves to the farm, constructing a dedicated **Heavy Maintenance & Forge Workshop** adjoining the barn or farmhouse.

## 6. Farm Partnership & Daily Support

When Gage moves to the farm as a committed partner, he brings his immense mechanical knowledge to bear on the farm’s physical systems:

- **Heavy Machine Overhaul:** Modifies farm machinery (processing kegs, seed makers, smelters, mills). All processing machines on the farm operate +15% faster and have zero risk of mechanical jam.
- **Structural Storm-Proofing:** Reinforces the framework of all farm buildings, sheds, and greenhouses. Farm structures take zero durability damage during violent radiation storms.
- **Tool Balancing & Ergonomics:** Tunes the player's primary farm tools (pickaxe, hoe, axe) with balanced pneumatic counterweights, reducing player stamina consumption by 15%.
- **Farm Routine:** Spends early mornings at his farm anvil sharpening blades, spends afternoons inspecting irrigation pumps and fence lines, and sits on the porch at dusk watching over the fields.

## 7. Faction Hub Unlocks (The Bastion)

Visiting the Bastion through Gage’s storyline opens unique facilities that adhere strictly to game design progression boundaries (no core tool gates; focused on heavy fabrication, structural architecture, and combat preparedness):

1. **The Heavy Metal Foundry & Forge:**
   - Players bring raw ores, scavenged girders, and scrap metal.
   - Unlocks recipes for heavy industrial decorative fencing, reinforced steel gates, metal roofing aesthetics, and exterior floodlight beacons.
2. **Defensive Perimeter & Readiness Workshop:**
   - Bastion sappers offer structural blueprints for reinforced farm fencing, barricades, and defensive perimeter floodlights that repel nocturnal curfew patrols.
   - Provides specialized hazard-protection modifications for player workwear (increased resistance to radiation storm exposure and physical impact).
3. **Scrap & Alloy Smelting Exchange:**
   - Trade bulky battlefield scrap and raw slag for refined structural steel beams, high-density tungsten ingots, and high-tensile fasteners.
4. **Narrative Investigation Hook — Frame Conversion Wing:**
   - In the secluded medical bunkers of the Bastion, the player can discover sealed operating theaters and classified dossiers detailing the Iron Creed’s experimental "frame conversions" on surviving humans.
   - This opens an optional investigation into the researchers and institutions responsible. Their work drew on the Creed's language of strength and survival, but the records do not establish that they belonged to its dominationist current.
   - The harmed people decide whether they want safety, medical support, privacy, testimony, justice, no further contact or another response. Gage can help carry out their chosen response and support specific accountability; he does not decide what they need or confront anyone without their wishes.

## 10. Seasonal & Festival Dialogue

Gage’s dialogue reflects his pragmatic mindset, mechanical vigilance, and dry assessments of pre-war domestic traditions.

### Daily Seasonal Dialogue
